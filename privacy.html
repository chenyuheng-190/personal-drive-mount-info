Privacy policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026.

Purpose and access

Personal Drive Mount is a utility operated by its account owner. It requests Google Drive access so the owner can list, read, create, update and remove their own files through a mounted directory. Each operation is performed by the locally running utility.

Where information is processed

The utility connects directly from the owner's computer or Colab runtime to Google's services. File content and metadata may be cached on that computer or runtime. OAuth credentials, configuration and diagnostic logs are stored there with restricted file permissions. This website receives neither Drive files nor OAuth credentials from the utility.

Sharing and retention

The utility has no separate hosted file collection service, analytics or advertising integration. Google processes requests under its own policies. The owner controls local files, caches and credentials, and is responsible for letting pending uploads finish before a temporary runtime is released.

Revoke access

The owner can revoke the utility's access in their Google Account third-party connections settings, disconnect the mounted directory, and remove local credentials and caches after all required uploads have finished. Revoking access does not delete files already stored in Drive.
